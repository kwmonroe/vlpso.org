Valor Leander Spirit Nights

Dine Out, Connect, and Support Our School!

What is a Spirit Night?

It is a fantastic partnership between our school and local restaurants. When you dine at partner locations on scheduled nights, a percentage of all sales goes directly back to school programs, funding essential resources, events, and student initiatives.

Making Connections!

Our Core Goal: Community Building! While raising funds is wonderful, the main objective of these events is to promote community building. This is a great way to meet with parents from your classroom and even with teachers or school leadership! Come out, share a meal, and strengthen our school family.

How It Works!

1. DINE OUT: visit our designated partner restaurant on the scheduled date with your family and friends

2. MENTION US: tell the cashier you are supporting Valor Leander so your bill is added to our total

3. ENTER THE RAFFLE: grab a photo of your family at the restaurant or a snapshot of your receipt

Earn Raffle Entries at Every Spirit Night! 🎟️

Each Spirit Night you attend gives you an extra entry into our End-of-Year Raffle! To claim your entry, simply fill out the form below with your name, email, and the Spirit Night location. Be sure to upload either a photo of your family enjoying the event or a snapshot of your receipt as proof of attendance. The more events you attend, the higher your chances of winning!
